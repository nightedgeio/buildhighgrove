Who you're dealing with

Highgrove Development Group LLC is a Texas family company — three brothers building new homes in Dallas–Fort Worth and Tulsa. We are principals, not agents: when we make you an offer, we are the buyer. Nothing is listed, nothing is shopped around, and your information stays with us.

We buy vacant lots, inherited properties, houses that need work, and land with back taxes or code issues. If the situation is complicated — an estate, several family members on the deed, unpaid liens — that is normal for us, and we'll walk through it with you honestly.

Straight answers

Is there any cost or obligation?

No. The offer is free, and "no" is a perfectly good answer. We'd rather you take our number and think it over than feel pushed.

Do I need to fix or clean anything?

No. We buy properties exactly as they sit — vacant, occupied, full of belongings, or overgrown.

What if the property was inherited or has several owners?

We work with estates and families often. Texas has clear paths for inherited property, and the title company handles the paperwork — we'll explain every step before you sign anything.

How fast can you close?

As fast as the title company can clear the paperwork — often a few weeks — or months from now if that suits you better. Your timeline.
